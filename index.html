1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: health inputs (e.g., height, weight, age, pregnancy details), symptoms for AI guidance, and preferences/settings.
  • Automatically collected: device/OS information and basic usage data; approximate location only if you grant permission.
  • Location: used to show nearby health facilities while the app is in use; not stored or shared.

Note: Permission-based information (like location) is only available if you explicitly allow it in your device settings.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide features (BMI, pregnancy tracking, AI guidance, maps/facilities).
  • Improve performance and reliability.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse.

3. Health Data Notice

  • Health data you enter is processed locally or temporarily for functionality.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • The App is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
Important: If you have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We may share limited information only if required by law or to operate essential services (e.g., maps APIs) under appropriate safeguards.

5. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your Choices

  • You can control permissions (location/notifications) in your device settings.
  • You may request deletion of stored data where applicable.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as Google Maps and Android system services, which have their own privacy policies.
