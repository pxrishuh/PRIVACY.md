Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Health Tools AI (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the “App”).

The App is intended for general wellness and lifestyle tracking only and is not a medical device.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: wellness inputs (e.g., height, weight, age), entries you add for tracking (e.g., water intake, goals, notes), and preferences/settings.
  • Automatically collected: basic device/OS information, app version, and basic usage events used to improve stability and performance.
  • Location (optional): if you grant permission, the App may access your approximate or precise location only while the app is in use to show nearby facilities or map results.

Permission-based information (like location) is only available if you explicitly enable it in your device settings.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide core functionality (e.g., BMI calculation for personal reference, hydration tracking, wellness goals, map/facility browsing).
  • Improve performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud or misuse.

3. Wellness Notice (Not Medical Advice)

  • The App provides general wellness tools and informational content for personal use.
  • We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and the App should not be used for medical decision-making.
  • Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
Disclaimer: This App is for general wellness and lifestyle tracking only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited information only:

  • With service providers that help operate the App (e.g., map providers) under appropriate safeguards.
  • If required by law or to protect users, the App, or the public (e.g., legal requests).

5. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as necessary to provide the App’s features and maintain security. You may delete app data by clearing the app storage on your device (or using any in-app delete options, if available).

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. However, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Choices

  • You can control permissions (e.g., location/notifications) in your device settings.
  • You can remove stored app data by clearing the App’s storage on your device.
  • You may contact us to request deletion guidance if applicable.

9. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services (for example, map APIs and Android system services) that may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page and reflected by the effective date.
